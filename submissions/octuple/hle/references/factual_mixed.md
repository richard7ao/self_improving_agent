# Factual, mixed, and uncertain adapter

For factual recall, identify the exact requested entity and compare only plausible
candidates. Audit qualifiers, dates, terminology, scope, and whether the answer is
remembered or inferred. Do not run irrelevant calculations to create activity; report
when no deterministic tool reduces the central uncertainty.

For mixed or uncertain tasks, state the competing domain interpretations and choose a
primary adapter from the governing semantics, not keywords. Use deterministic helpers
only for separable calculations. Keep unresolved domain or recall risk in confidence.
