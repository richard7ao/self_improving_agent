# Chemistry adapter

Identify species, structures, conditions, phase, and requested property. Select and
justify the reaction or model before calculation. Balance atoms and charge, compute
quantities, then review limiting reagent, oxidation state, stereochemistry, isomers,
kinetic versus thermodynamic control, solvent, catalyst, temperature, phase, units,
and significant figures.

Use `scripts/chem_check.py` for formula parsing, molar mass, balance/charge checks,
stoichiometry, limiting reagent, concentration arithmetic, oxidation consistency, and
significant figures. It validates consequences of a selected reaction; it cannot
choose the reaction, mechanism, product, phase, or stereochemistry.
