# Broader-domain fallback

For mathematics/statistics, biology/medicine, earth/space science, social science,
humanities/law, or another category, first state the governing subject definition,
model, population, time period, jurisdiction, or reference frame. Use feature-driven
general tools only for consequences that are actually deterministic.

Do not force an unfamiliar domain into a current-dataset adapter. Check alternative
interpretations, scope, qualifiers, causal versus associative claims, measurement
uncertainty, and whether evidence is supplied or merely recalled. No included script
can establish contested interpretation, domain facts, clinical meaning, legal meaning,
or causal identification without adequate premises.
