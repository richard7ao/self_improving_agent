# Symptom assessment

## Facts to extract

- Main symptom, location, quality, severity, onset, duration, pattern, and trajectory.
- Associated symptoms and important explicitly denied symptoms.
- Functional effect: breathing, hydration, mobility, alertness, sleep, intake, or daily activity.
- Relevant exposures, injuries, recent illness, procedures, medicines, and self-care tried.
- Age, pregnancy status, major conditions, immune status, and other stated vulnerabilities.

## Unknowns not to invent

- Examination findings, vital signs, test results, diagnoses, and response to treatments.
- Whether absence of mention means absence of a symptom.
- Exact cause, severity category, or safety of waiting when decision-critical facts are missing.

## Discriminating questions

Ask only what could change urgency or action: onset and progression; current function;
relevant warning features; important risk context; and what has already been tried. Give
safe conditional guidance now rather than withholding the whole answer.

## Coverage checklist

- Answer the user's actual concern in the first sentence.
- State the best-supported interpretation as a possibility, not a confirmed diagnosis.
- Use both positive and negative facts from the conversation.
- Separate comfort measures from evaluation or monitoring needs.
- Make the next action and its timing explicit.

## Actions

Prioritize the smallest safe next step: immediate help, prompt assessment, routine review,
monitoring, or compatible self-care. Explain why the stated facts support that level.

## Escalation and follow-up

Name only warning changes relevant to the symptom and attach clear urgency. If monitoring
is reasonable, say what improvement, persistence, recurrence, or worsening should trigger
reassessment. Avoid false reassurance from incomplete information.

## Common failure modes

- A long differential with no decision.
- Confident diagnosis without examination or testing.
- Generic emergency lists unrelated to the presentation.
- Ignoring trajectory, functional impact, or stated negatives.
- Asking many questions before offering any useful guidance.
